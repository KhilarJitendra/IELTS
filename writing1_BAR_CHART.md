# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 – Bar Chart Template

**Target:** Band 7–8 | **Length:** Aim for 160–170 words (minimum 150) | **Time:** 20 minutes

Use four paragraphs: **Introduction → Overall → Body Paragraph 1 → Body Paragraph 2**. No separate conclusion.

## 1. Introduction (20–30 words)

**Purpose:** Paraphrase the question without opinions or detailed figures.

**Template:**

The bar chart provides information about + **[topic]** + **[place]** + **[time period]**.

**Example:**

> The bar chart provides information about technological innovation rates across six different regions in 2020.

**Tense:** Present simple to introduce the chart (*provides*), even if the data is historical.

## 2. Overall (30–40 words)

**Purpose:** Summarise the most noticeable features. Generally avoid exact figures here.

**Template:**

Overall, it is clear that + **[highest/main feature]** + whereas/while + **[lowest/second main feature]**.

**Example:**

> Overall, it is clear that North America recorded the highest technological innovation rate, whereas Africa had the lowest. Europe also demonstrated a relatively high rate compared with the remaining regions.

**Tense:** Match the data period; past simple for 2020 (*recorded, had*).

## 3. Body Paragraph 1 (45–55 words)

**Purpose:** Describe the first group of important data with figures and comparisons.

**Templates:**

- **Looking at the details,** + **[category and value]**.
- **The highest figure was recorded in** + **[category]** + at + **[value]**.
- **This was followed by** + **[next-highest category]** + at + **[value]**.
- **In comparison,** + **[another category and comparison]**.

**Example:**

> Looking at the details, the highest technological innovation rate was recorded in North America, at 8%. This was followed by Europe, at 7%. In comparison, Asia registered a lower rate of 5%, which was three percentage points below the figure for North America.

**Tense:** Past simple for 2020 (*was recorded, registered*).

## 4. Body Paragraph 2 (45–55 words)

**Purpose:** Describe the remaining figures and make meaningful comparisons.

**Templates:**

- **By contrast,** + **[lowest/contrasting category and value]**.
- **Meanwhile,** + **[another category and value]**.
- **Similarly,** + **[category with a similar pattern or value]**.
- **Whereas** + **[category A and value]** + **[contrasting category B and value]**.

**Example:**

> By contrast, Africa recorded the lowest technological innovation rate, at just 3%. Meanwhile, South America and Australia registered rates of 4% and 6%, respectively. Australia's figure was twice as high as Africa's, whereas South America's rate was only slightly higher than that of Africa.

**Tense:** Past simple for 2020 (*recorded, registered, was*).

---

## Tense reference

| Data period | Recommended tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past years | Past simple | The figure **increased**. |
| Current data / no specified date | Present simple (when appropriate) | The figure **is** the highest. |
| Future forecasts | Prediction language | The figure **is expected to rise**. |
| Past and future | Mixed tenses | It **rose** and **is projected to rise**. |

The introduction may use present simple to describe what the chart shows, while the data description uses the appropriate past, present, or future tense.

## Quick memorisation template

**1. Introduction**  
The bar chart provides information about + **[topic]** + **[place]** + **[time period]**.

**2. Overall**  
Overall, it is clear that + **[main feature]** + whereas/while + **[second main feature]**.

**3. Body Paragraph 1**  
Looking at the details, + **[highest category and value]**. This was followed by + **[next category and value]**. In comparison, + **[another comparison]**.

**4. Body Paragraph 2**  
By contrast, + **[remaining category and value]**. Meanwhile, + **[another category and value]**. Whereas + **[final comparison]**.

**Notes:** The plus signs indicate how to assemble the sentence; do not write them in the exam. Place and time are optional if not given. Choose only expressions that fit the data. The example figures are illustrative. A separate conclusion is not needed for Academic Task 1.
