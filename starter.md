# IELTS 34-Day Intensive Study Plan

## Goal

Target Overall Band: **8.0**

Target individual scores:

- Listening: **8.5–9.0**
- Reading: **8.5**
- Writing: **7.0–7.5**
- Speaking: **7.5–8.0**

Daily study capacity: **~6.5 hours**

---

# PLAN OVERVIEW

## Phase 1 — Learn IELTS
**Days 1–8**

- Days 1–2: Listening
- Days 3–4: Reading
- Days 5–6: Writing
- Days 7–8: Speaking

Goal:

> Understand the exam, understand every major question type, try examples, and identify strengths and weaknesses.

---

## Phase 2 — Fix Weaknesses + Vocabulary
**Days 9–11**

Goal:

> Work specifically on the problems discovered during Days 1–8 and start building personal vocabulary lists.

---

## Phase 3 — Intensive Practice
**Days 12–18**

Daily target:

- 2 Listening modules
- 2 Reading modules
- 2 Writing Task 1 answers
- 2 Writing Task 2 essays
- 1.5 hours Speaking
- Vocabulary revision morning + night

Goal:

> Practice heavily AND understand every mistake.

---

## Phase 4 — Exam Mode
**Days 19–34**

Goal:

> Complete full IELTS simulations under strict exam conditions and build consistency, endurance, accuracy and confidence.

---

# PHASE 1 — DAYS 1–8

# Day 1 — Listening Fundamentals

## Goal

Understand how IELTS Listening works and explore the easier/common question types.

## Learn

Understand:

- Number of sections
- Number of questions
- How the audio works
- When answers appear
- Spelling rules
- Singular/plural answers
- Word limits
- Distractors

## Practice

Explore:

### Form Completion

Example:

Name: John Smith  
Arrival date: ______  
Number of guests: ______

### Note Completion

Example:

The library opens at ______.

### Sentence Completion

Example:

Students must submit their assignments before ______.

### Table Completion

Practice several small sets.

## Track

Example:

| Question Type | Result | Difficulty |
|---|---:|---|
| Form Completion | 8/10 | Easy |
| Note Completion | 7/10 | Medium |
| Sentence Completion | 6/10 | Medium |
| Table Completion | 8/10 | Easy |

Do NOT worry about your band score yet.

The objective is discovery.

---

# Day 3 — Reading Fundamentals

## Goal

Understand IELTS Reading and explore the first major question types.

Learn:

- Reading format
- Number of passages
- Number of questions
- 60-minute time limit
- Skimming
- Scanning
- Keywords
- Synonyms/paraphrasing

Explore:

- True / False / Not Given
- Yes / No / Not Given
- Sentence Completion
- Summary Completion

## Example — True / False / Not Given

Passage:

> The university opened its first overseas campus in Canada in 2015.

Statement:

> The university opened a Canadian campus in 2015.

Answer:

**TRUE**

If the passage said 2017:

**FALSE**

If the passage didn't provide the year:

**NOT GIVEN**

# Day 5 — Writing Task 1

For Academic IELTS.

## Learn

Understand:

- Task 1 requirements
- Minimum 150 words
- Approximately 20 minutes
- Introduction
- Overview
- Details
- Comparisons

Explore:

- Line graph
- Bar chart
- Pie chart
- Table
- Process
- Map
- Mixed charts

You do NOT need to master every type today.

Understand how they work and study examples.

## Basic Structure

### Introduction

Paraphrase the question.

### Overview

Describe the biggest/main trends.

### Body 1

Important details and comparisons.

### Body 2

Remaining important details and comparisons.

## Example

Instead of:

> Sales went up a lot.

Write:

> Sales increased significantly between 2010 and 2020.

Write at least **one complete Task 1 answer**.

---

# Day 6 — Writing Task 2

## Learn

Understand:

- Minimum 250 words
- Approximately 40 minutes
- Task Response
- Coherence and Cohesion
- Lexical Resource
- Grammatical Range and Accuracy

Explore common essay types:

- Opinion
- Discuss Both Views
- Advantages/Disadvantages
- Problems/Solutions
- Two-Part Questions

## Simple Structure

### Introduction

Paraphrase topic + answer question.

### Body Paragraph 1

Main idea  
Explanation  
Example

### Body Paragraph 2

Main idea  
Explanation  
Example

### Conclusion

Summarize your position.

## Example Topic

> Some people believe working from home is beneficial, while others believe employees should work from offices. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Write at least **one complete Task 2 essay**.

# Day 7 — Speaking Parts 1 & 2

## Speaking Part 1

Practice topics such as:

- Work
- Study
- Home
- Hometown
- Technology
- Friends
- Food
- Exercise
- Travel
- Weekends

Example:

Question:

> Do you enjoy exercising?

Weak answer:

> Yes. I like exercise.

Better natural answer:

> Yes, definitely. I go to the gym regularly because exercise helps me stay active and also gives me a break from work.

Do NOT memorize answers.

---

## Speaking Part 2

You'll receive a cue card.

Example:

> Describe a city you would like to visit.

You should talk about:

- What city it is
- Where it is
- Why you want to visit
- What you would do there

Prepare briefly and speak for up to **2 minutes**.

Record yourself.

Check:

- Long pauses
- Repetition
- Grammar
- Vocabulary
- Pronunciation
- Fluency

# HOW TO REVIEW LISTENING

Suppose:

Score:

**30/40**

Don't stop there.

Analyze all 10 incorrect answers.

Example:

| Question | Mistake | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Q7 | students/student | Plural |
| Q12 | Wrong answer | Distractor |
| Q19 | Wrong answer | Didn't recognize synonym |
| Q25 | Wrong MCQ | Lost concentration |
| Q33 | accommodation | Spelling |

Then determine:

> What exactly is costing me marks?

---

# HOW TO REVIEW READING

Example:

Score:

**29/40**

Analyze:

- 4 mistakes → T/F/NG
- 3 mistakes → Matching Information
- 2 mistakes → Vocabulary
- 2 mistakes → Time pressure

Conclusion:

> T/F/NG needs targeted practice.

---

# HOW TO REVIEW WRITING

After every essay check:

1. Did I answer the question?
2. Are my ideas clear?
3. Are paragraphs logical?
4. Did I explain my ideas?
5. Did I provide examples where appropriate?
6. Did I repeat vocabulary?
7. Are there grammar mistakes?
8. Did I stay within time?

---

# HOW TO PRACTICE SPEAKING

Practice:

- Part 1
- Part 2
- Part 3

Record yourself.

Example Part 2:

> Describe a skill you would like to learn.

Speak for up to 2 minutes.

Listen to your recording.

Find:

- Long pauses
- "umm"
- "like"
- "you know"
- repeated words
- grammar errors
- unclear pronunciation

# ==================================================
# VOCABULARY RULES
# ==================================================

## Rule 1 — Don't use difficult words just to impress the examiner

Simple + correct is better than complicated + incorrect.

---

## Rule 2 — Learn vocabulary in context

Don't memorize:

> alleviate = reduce

Learn:

> alleviate traffic congestion

Example:

> Better public transportation could alleviate traffic congestion.

---

## Rule 3 — Learn collocations

Don't only learn:

> significant

Learn:

> significant increase  
> significant impact  
> significant improvement  
> significant difference

---

## Rule 4 — Create your own sentence

Whenever you add a word, write your own example.

---

## Rule 5 — Don't blindly replace simple words

For example:

"beneficial" does NOT replace "good" everywhere.

Correct:

> Regular exercise is beneficial for physical health.

Natural Speaking:

> I had a great weekend.

Not:

> I had a beneficial weekend.
